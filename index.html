Education: Enjoy five group golf lessons covering essential golf fundamentals and four educational sessions, focused on golf etiquette, scoring, rules, handicaps, and core golf fundamentals, so you understand the game, build confidence, and know how to move forward in your golf journey.

 

Networking Opportunities: Connect with other accomplished women in various industries through our networking events.
